## Key Learning Points

### Why Daily Protection Matters
- UV damage is the #1 cause of premature ageing
- UVA (silent killer) penetrates deep, causes cancer
- UVB causes visible burning and inflammation

### Understanding SPF
- SPF 15 blocks 93% of UVB
- SPF 30 blocks 97%
- Higher SPF doesn't mean better protection
