## Key Learning Points

### Eye Area Challenges
- Thinnest skin on the face
- First area to show ageing signs
- Requires specialized formulations

### Common Ingredients
- Matrixyl® 3000 (peptide anti-wrinkle complex)
- Chicory Root & Tara Tree (tightening complex)
- Hesperidin Methyl Chalcone (anti-puffiness)
- Chrysin (anti-dark circles)

### Application Tips
- Use morning and night for cumulative effect
- Apply under-eye products to lower eye area and crow's feet
- Use eye opener serum on upper eyelids
