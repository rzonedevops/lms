## DIRECTIONS FOR USE

### POST PEEL
On the evening following a RégimA Peel apply to treated area as desired, especially if there is visible peeling, massaging gently. 'The Blue Gel' may be used in combination with RégimA Scar treatment. Continue for a couple of nights following the peel, or until any peeling has ceased. Recommence RégimA home care essentials.

### POST LASER / DERMABRASION
Following procedure apply as directed by a doctor or RégimA skin care professional. Continue use until all laser or dermabrasion skin debris has cleared and skin is free of any peeling or crusting. (Do not pick or pull off any skin, it will slough off naturally when ready). Follow with RégimA post therapy home care.
