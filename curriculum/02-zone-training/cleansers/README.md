## Key Learning Points

### Why Cleansing Matters
- First step in any skincare routine
- Prepares skin for active ingredients
- Low foam formulations ensure actives stay on skin

### Common Ingredients
- Ruby Star Grapefruit (anti-bacterial, toning)
- Bisabolol (anti-inflammatory, absorption enhancer)
- Centella Asiatica (healing, collagen synthesis)
