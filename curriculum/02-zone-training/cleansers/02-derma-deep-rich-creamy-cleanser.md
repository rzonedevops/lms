# Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser

## Product Overview

A Super Rich moisturizing creamy cleanser, containing rosehip and blackcurrant fruit oils, rich in vitamins, anti-oxidants and essential fatty acids, deeply hydrates whilst thoroughly cleansing.

The low foam design ensures that all ingredients immediately apply a film of activity on the skin, leaving it clean, yet soft, and velvety smooth with a layer of essential moisture.

## DIRECTIONS FOR USE

**AM & PM:** Wet hands, face and neck and using one pump depression of cleanser, massage gently for one minute, avoiding the eye area if sensitive. Rinse off thoroughly.

To be followed by the appropriate RégimA eye products, serums, and day or night home care.

*(If the product enters the eyes, rinse out thoroughly with plenty of water)*

**pH of product: 6.48**
